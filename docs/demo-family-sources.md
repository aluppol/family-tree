# Darwin–Wedgwood demo dataset: sources and verification

The demo family lives in [`backend/family_tree/adapters/demo/darwin_wedgwood.ged`](../backend/family_tree/adapters/demo/darwin_wedgwood.ged): GEDCOM 5.5.1, UTF-8, **65 people** (`@I1@`–`@I65@`) and **22 families** (`@F1@`–`@F22@`). Every guest sandbox is seeded from it.

Everyone in the file is long deceased. The latest birth is William Robert Darwin (1894) and the latest death is Nora Barlow (1989). The research was done on 23 September 2026.

The data shows the family as a graph. Charles Darwin married his first cousin Emma Wedgwood, and his sister Caroline married Emma's brother Josiah Wedgwood III. Leonard Darwin's second wife, Mildred, was a great-granddaughter of Josiah Wedgwood II. Charles Langton married a Wedgwood first (Charlotte) and then a Darwin (Catherine). Erasmus Darwin, Charles Langton, Francis Darwin and Leonard Darwin each have more than one marriage in the file.

## How dates and places were chosen

1. **Day-level dates** are used only when an authoritative source gives them and no comparable source contradicts them. The authoritative sources are listed in the key below. Genealogy sites (marked `*`: WikiTree, Find a Grave, FamilySearch, The Peerage) only corroborate. When one of them is the only source for a day, the GEDCOM gives the year (or month) and this file records the unverified day.
2. **When authoritative sources disagree on the day**, the GEDCOM uses the precision they agree on. For example, Hensleigh Wedgwood's death is `JUN 1891` because the sources give both 1 June and 2 June. Every such case is listed under "Discrepancies".
3. **Primary sources were preferred.** Emma Darwin's own diaries and her daughter Henrietta Litchfield's edition of the family letters decided several conflicts between secondary sources.
4. **Pre-1752 English dates are Old Style (Julian), as the sources give them.** This applies to Erasmus Darwin (12 Dec 1731), Josiah Wedgwood (baptised 12 Jul 1730) and Mary Howard (12 Feb 1740). They were not converted.
5. **Baptism-only births** use `ABT` with the baptism date: Josiah Wedgwood and John Wedgwood. `ABT 1834` for Arthur Vaughan Williams is derived from his memorial ("aged 40").
6. **Places** are included only when a source states them or the person's documented residence makes them clear (flagged in the notes column). Places use historic counties (for example Sedbergh, Yorkshire) and run from small to large, ending with the country. `PLAC` is omitted when nothing could be verified.
7. **NAME** uses the birth surname. This gives `Charlotte Mildred /Langton/`: she was born a Langton and her family took the name Massingberd by royal licence in 1887. There are three people named `Josiah /Wedgwood/` (I, II and III), distinguished by their NOTE lines and dates.
